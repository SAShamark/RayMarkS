Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

RayMarkS Games (“we”, “us”, or “our”) develops and publishes Births Day Keeper (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

Information Stored on Your Device

The App is local-first. It stores the information you add to manage birthday reminders on your device, including names, birthdays, optional notes, reminder schedules, sections, and App settings. This information is used to provide the App's core functionality and local notifications.

We do not operate our own server that receives this information as part of the App's standard local functionality.

Contacts

If you choose to use the contact-import feature, the App requests read-only access to your device contacts. It reads contact names and birthday events to show possible birthdays for import. Contact information is processed on your device and is not sent to us for this feature. You can decline or revoke contact permission in your device settings.

Google Drive Backups

The Google Drive backup feature is optional. If you choose to connect a Google Account and authorize the feature, the App requests the Google Drive drive.file permission. This permits the App to create, read, update, and delete only the Drive files it uses with the App.

A backup can include the birthday and reminder information described above. The backup is stored in your Google Drive account in a folder created for the App. Google processes Google Account and Drive data under its own privacy policy. You can disconnect Google Drive in the App and delete the backup file from your Google Drive account.

Advertising

The free version of the App uses Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial advertisements. AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and process information such as advertising and device identifiers, IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and approximate location inferred from IP address. This information may be used to provide, measure, personalise where permitted, and protect advertising from fraud.

The App uses Google's consent tools where required before requesting ads. Your device may also provide controls to reset or restrict its advertising identifier. These controls do not necessarily stop all contextual advertising or diagnostic processing.

Subscriptions and Purchases

The App offers optional Premium subscriptions. Payments are processed by Google Play on Android and the Apple App Store on iOS. We do not collect or store your payment card information.

The App uses Adapty to present subscription offers, validate purchases, and determine Premium access. Depending on the SDK configuration and your platform, Adapty may process purchase and subscription status, product information, device and app information, IP address, and advertising identifiers. Adapty does not receive the birthday information you store in the App unless we explicitly send it, which the App does not do.

Service Providers and Data Sharing

Information described in this Policy may be processed by the following service providers to deliver their respective services:

  • Google — Google Sign-In, Google Drive, Google Play, and AdMob;
  • Google Mobile Ads — advertising;
  • Adapty — subscription infrastructure and purchase validation;
  • Google Play and Apple — payment processing, according to their applicable privacy policies.

These providers may process information outside your country of residence. We may also disclose information where required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, and security.

Retention and Deletion

You can delete locally stored App information by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App. This does not automatically delete information already processed by third-party providers, such as advertising, subscription, payment, or cloud-backup providers.

Data held by those providers is retained according to their settings, retention practices, and legal obligations. You can delete Google Drive backups from your Drive account. To request access, correction, or deletion of information we can identify and control, contact us at raymarksgames@gmail.com.

Your Choices

You may choose not to grant contact or Google Drive permissions. You may manage notification, contact, and advertising-identifier permissions in device settings. Subscription cancellation and refund options are managed through the store from which you purchased the subscription.

Children's Privacy

The App's intended audience is identified in its store listing. We do not ask users to provide their real name, address, or contact information to use the App. The App includes third-party advertising and subscription services that may automatically process information as described above. If you believe that a child’s information has been processed inappropriately, contact us so we can investigate.

Changes

We may update this Policy when the App or its information practices change. Updates will appear on this page with a revised date.
