Word Keeper

Account & Data Deletion

Word Keeper does not create its own account — it only connects your existing Google account for the optional Google Drive backup feature.

Disconnect Your Google Account

Open Word Keeper → Settings → Google Drive → Disconnect.

Delete Your Backup Data

Your backup file is stored in your own Google Drive (My Drive / WordKeeper folder). You can delete it at any time directly from Google Drive. Word Keeper does not retain a copy anywhere else.

Delete All App Data

Uninstalling Word Keeper permanently removes all locally stored data — including words, sections, and progress — from your device.
