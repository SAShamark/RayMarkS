Word Keeper

Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Word Keeper works without an account. If you sign in (with Google, Apple, or through Telegram), Word Keeper creates an account on its own server to sync your dictionary between devices and to power friends, shared word sets, and the leaderboard. This page explains how to delete that account and its data.

Delete Your Account in the App

Open Word Keeper, signed in, and go to Settings → Account → Delete account, then confirm. In the Telegram Mini App the same option is in Settings.

The account and all its data are deleted from our server immediately, and from your other devices the next time they sync.

Request Deletion Without the App

Email raymarksgames@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my account”. Include the email address you sign in with (Google or Apple) or your Telegram username so we can find the account. We delete it within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What Is Deleted

  • Your account: name, nickname, avatar, sign-in links to Google, Apple, or Telegram, and settings.
  • Learning profiles, words, translations, sections, practice history, and achievements.
  • Friends, word sets you sent and received, and your place on the leaderboard.
  • Certificates: their numbers can no longer be verified.

What Is Kept

Records of purchases made with Telegram Stars (amount, date, payment ID, and the payer’s Telegram ID) are kept without a link to the deleted account, for accounting and refunds, for as long as the law requires.

Word sets you sent disappear from your friends’ received lists. Words a friend already added to their own dictionary stay with them.

Data Outside Our Server

Without an account, your dictionary is stored only on your device. Uninstalling Word Keeper or clearing its data removes it permanently.

Google Drive backup (optional) is stored in your own Google Drive (My Drive / WordKeeper folder). Deleting your account does not delete it: remove the file directly from Google Drive. To stop backups, open Settings → Google Drive → Disconnect.

Anonymous technical records used to protect the translation service and anonymous analytics are described in the Privacy Policy; to request their deletion, email the address above.
