Word Keeper

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Word Keeper (the “app”) collects, processes, and protects information when you use it — in the Android and iOS apps and in the Word Keeper Mini App in Telegram.

Using Word Keeper Without an Account

You can use Word Keeper without signing in. Your words, translations, sections, learning profiles, settings, and practice progress are then stored only on your device. Deleting the app or clearing its data removes them.

Your Word Keeper Account

You can sign in with Google or Apple in the mobile apps. In Telegram, the Mini App signs you in automatically with your Telegram account. Signing in creates an account on the Word Keeper server (api.wordkeeper.cc), operated by us.

When you sign in we receive:

  • Google or Apple: an account identifier, your email address, and your name (Apple may give us a private relay address instead of your real email).
  • Telegram: your Telegram user ID, first and last name, username, profile photo link, and language setting.

With an account, the following is stored on our server and synced between your devices: learning profiles, words and translations, sections, practice sessions and answers, review progress, achievements, app settings, your nickname, avatar, and frame. We use this data only to provide the app’s features.

Friends, Shared Word Sets, and the Leaderboard

These features are available only with an account.

  • Friends. Each account has a short friend code. When you add someone (by code or invite link), you both become friends. Friends can see your nickname, avatar, frame, learning languages, word count, streak, the date you joined, and your achievements.
  • Shared word sets. You can send a set of words with translations, an optional title, and a comment to your friends. The set is stored on our server and delivered to the friends you choose. In Telegram, the Word Keeper bot may send them a message that you shared words.
  • Leaderboard. Weekly practice points are calculated from your completed practice sessions. The leaderboard shows your nickname, avatar, frame, learning languages, and points to all Word Keeper users with an account, and your profile card (word count, streak, join date, achievements) can be opened from it.

Translation

Word Keeper uses Google Cloud Translation. When you request a translation, the word or phrase and the selected languages are sent through Firebase Cloud Functions to Google Cloud Translation. Google states that submitted text is used only to provide the service: cloud.google.com/translate/data-usage.

To reduce repeated requests, Word Keeper may store the translated result, language codes, creation time, and a hashed value of the original text in Cloud Firestore. The original text is not stored in plain text, and cached translations are not linked to your name, email, or account.

Google ML Kit may translate on the device instead; then the text stays on your device. Language models may be downloaded from Google.

Pronunciation and Speech

Listening. To read words aloud, the app may use your device’s text-to-speech or send the word and its language to the Word Keeper server, which generates the audio. These requests are not stored with your words.

Speaking exercises. With your permission, the app uses the microphone and your device’s speech recognition service (provided by Apple or Google) to check pronunciation. We do not receive or store recordings; the speech service may process audio under Apple’s or Google’s privacy policy.

Purchases

In Telegram, Premium and certificates are paid with Telegram Stars. We receive the payment ID, amount, date, the purchased item, and your Telegram ID, and store them to grant the purchase, handle refunds, and keep accounting records. Payment details are processed by Telegram; we never receive card information.

The mobile apps do not currently sell anything.

Certificates

If you get a level certificate, it shows the name you enter, the language, the level, and a certificate number. Anyone with the number can verify that the certificate is genuine.

Anonymous Authentication and Service Protection

To protect the translation service, the app creates an anonymous Firebase identifier. Firebase Authentication, Firebase App Check, and Google Play Integrity (on Android) may process this identifier, IP address, app and device information, installation identifiers, and integrity tokens — only to authenticate requests, prevent abuse, and enforce usage limits. Technical request and character counters are linked to this anonymous identifier and contain no words or personal details.

Optional Google Drive Backup

If you enable backup, the app connects to your Google account with the limited drive.file permission, which gives access only to backup files created by the app. The backup may contain your words and translations, sections, learning profiles, settings, and progress, and is stored in your own Google Drive. The email address and basic profile received for this are used only to show the connected account and are not stored on our server for this purpose.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device. In Telegram, the Word Keeper bot may send you messages about shared word sets and purchases.

Analytics and Crash Reporting

Word Keeper uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the app is used and to fix problems. They may collect app usage events, practice statistics (such as exercise type and whether an answer was correct), interactions with reminders and the mini window, device model, operating system and app version, installation identifier, and crash reports. They do not include your words, translations, section names, or imported files, and are linked to an anonymous installation identifier rather than your name, email, or account.

Advertising and Tracking

Word Keeper does not show advertising, does not use the advertising ID, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with service providers needed to run the app — Google and Firebase services, Apple (Sign in with Apple, speech recognition), Telegram (sign-in, bot messages, Stars payments), and the hosting provider of our server — and, as described above, with your friends and other users through friends and the leaderboard.

Data Retention and Deletion

Account data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Account → Delete account; its data is then deleted from our server immediately. Purchase records are kept without a link to the account for accounting and refunds as required by law. Details and how to request deletion without the app: Account & Data Deletion.

Data stored only on your device is removed when you delete the app or clear its data. Google Drive backups must be deleted from your Google Drive. Cached translations and anonymous technical records may remain until removed during service maintenance; to request deletion, contact us.

Security

Data between the app and our server is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to the server is limited to what is needed to run the service.

Children’s Privacy

Word Keeper is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or its data processing changes. Updates are published on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

Contact
