Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

RayMarkS Games ("we", "us", or "our") develops and publishes Arrow Gallery: Art Puzzle (the "Game"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and disclosed when you use the Game.

Locally Stored Information

The Game stores game progress, earned rewards, completed levels, settings, and your chosen profile name and avatar locally on your device.

Analytics events may separately describe gameplay activity, such as level starts, completions, and use of game features. We do not intentionally include your profile name or selected avatar in these events.

Analytics and Diagnostics

The Game uses Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, and Unity Analytics to understand gameplay, measure stability, investigate errors, and improve performance.

These services may process game and session events, app interactions, installation identifiers, device and operating-system information, app version, network information, and crash reports. Crash reports may include stack traces and relevant application state.

Attribution and Purchase Measurement

The Game uses Tenjin to measure installations, sessions, and purchases and to understand which marketing campaigns bring players to the Game.

Depending on SDK configuration and applicable privacy choices, Tenjin may process advertising and installation identifiers, IP address, device and app information, attribution information, and purchase details.

For Google Play purchases, the Game sends Tenjin the product identifier, price, currency, purchase receipt, and receipt signature for purchase validation and measurement.

Advertising

The Game uses CAS.AI (Clever Ads Solutions) and its integrated advertising partners to deliver advertisements, including optional rewarded advertisements.

These services may process advertising and device identifiers, IP address, device and app information, approximate location inferred from IP address, and information about advertising impressions and interactions. This information may be used to deliver and measure advertisements, support personalization where permitted, and prevent fraud.

The advertising partners involved and the information processed depend on the released SDK configuration, region, and applicable privacy choices.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Google Play. We do not collect or store payment card information. Purchase information may also be processed by our analytics and attribution providers as described above. Google handles payment-related information under its own privacy policy.

Providers and Disclosures

Information described in this Policy may be processed by Google/Firebase, Unity, Tenjin, CAS.AI, and advertising partners integrated into the Game.

Their privacy information is available here:

  • Firebase privacy information
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Unity Privacy Policy
  • Tenjin Privacy Policy
  • CAS.AI Privacy Policy

If you contact us by email, we process your email address and message to respond to your request. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect legal rights.

Providers may process information outside your country of residence.

Retention and Deletion

Clearing the Game's app data or uninstalling it removes locally stored game data. This does not automatically delete information already transmitted to service providers.

Other information is retained according to its purpose, applicable legal requirements, and provider settings and retention practices.

To request access, correction, or deletion of information associated with your use of the Game, contact raymarksgames@gmail.com. We may need additional information to locate relevant records and verify your request. Do not send passwords or payment card details.

Privacy Choices

The Game does not currently provide a general switch to disable all analytics and diagnostic services.

Your device may provide controls to delete or restrict its advertising identifier. These controls do not necessarily stop all analytics, contextual advertising, or diagnostic collection.

Where available, privacy forms provided by advertising services explain additional choices. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to object to processing, withdraw consent, or request restrictions. Contact us to submit a request.

Uninstalling the Game stops further collection by that installation but does not erase previously transmitted information.

Children's Privacy

The Game's intended audience is identified in Google Play. We do not ask players to provide their real name, address, or contact information to play.

The Game includes third-party services that may automatically process information as described above. If you believe a child's personal information has been processed inappropriately, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Changes

We may update this Policy when the Game or its information practices change. Updates will appear on this page with a revised date.
