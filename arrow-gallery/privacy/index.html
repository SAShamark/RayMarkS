Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

RayMarkS Games ("we", "us", or "our") develops and publishes Arrow Gallery: Art Puzzle (the "Game"). This Privacy Policy explains how the Game handles information.

Information We Collect

The Game stores the following information locally on your device to provide its features:

  • game progress, earned rewards, and completed levels;
  • game settings, including music, sound, and vibration preferences;
  • a profile name and selected avatar, if you choose to set them.

These local records are separate from the analytics and diagnostic information described below.

Analytics and Diagnostics

The Game uses Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, and Unity Analytics. These services may automatically process information such as game and session events, app interactions, installation or app-instance identifiers, device model and operating-system information, app version, approximate network information, and crash or diagnostic reports.

Crash reports may include stack traces, relevant application state, and device metadata at the time of a crash. Analytics and diagnostics are used to understand how the Game is used, measure stability, investigate errors, and improve gameplay and performance.

We do not intentionally send your locally stored game progress, profile name, or avatar to these services unless a feature is configured to do so. The exact information processed can depend on the SDK versions and their configuration in the released version of the Game.

Purchases

If the Game offers in-app purchases, payment processing is handled by Google Play. We do not collect or store payment card information. Please review Google's privacy policy for information about how Google handles purchase-related data.

Service Providers and Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information described above may be processed by Google and Firebase in connection with Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, and by Unity in connection with Unity Analytics, to provide analytics and diagnostic services. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

  • Firebase privacy information
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Unity Privacy Policy

Data Retention and Deletion

Uninstalling the Game or clearing its app data removes locally stored game data from your device. It does not automatically delete information already processed by Firebase, Google, or Unity.

Analytics and diagnostic information is retained according to the applicable service configuration and provider retention practices, for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above. To request deletion of information that we can identify and control, contact us at raymarksgames@gmail.com. We may need additional technical details to locate a record associated with an installation.

Children's Privacy

The Game's intended age group is stated in its Google Play listing. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children beyond the automatic technical and diagnostic information described in this Policy. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us so that we can review the request.

Consent and Your Choices

Analytics and diagnostic collection is enabled when the Game runs. The Game does not currently provide an in-app setting to turn these services off. Consent requirements vary by jurisdiction; where prior consent is required by applicable law, an appropriate consent mechanism or SDK configuration may be required before distribution in that jurisdiction. You may stop further collection by uninstalling the Game. This does not remove information already processed by third-party service providers.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game changes. Any update will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at raymarksgames@gmail.com.
