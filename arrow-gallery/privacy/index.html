Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

RayMarkS Games ("we", "us", or "our") develops and publishes Arrow Gallery: Art Puzzle (the "Game"). This Privacy Policy explains how the Game handles information.

Information We Collect

The Game does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information with RayMarkS Games or third parties.

The Game may store the following information locally on your device to provide its features:

  • game progress, earned rewards, and completed levels;
  • game settings, including music, sound, and vibration preferences;
  • a profile name and selected avatar, if you choose to set them.

This information remains on your device and is not sent to our servers.

Purchases

If the Game offers in-app purchases, payment processing is handled by Google Play. We do not collect or store payment card information. Please review Google's privacy policy for information about how Google handles purchase-related data.

Advertising and Analytics

The current version of the Game does not use third-party advertising networks or analytics services.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the Game does not transmit personal information to us, we do not maintain personal information about players on our servers.

Data Deletion

You can delete locally stored game data by using the clear-data option in your device settings or by uninstalling the Game.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game changes. Any update will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at raymarksgames@gmail.com.
