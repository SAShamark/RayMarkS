Independent game development

Small studio. Full production focus.

Markovskiy Games is an independent game-development practice led by Oleksandr Markovskiy — a Unity and C# developer with commercial experience building games and gameplay systems.

The focus is on clear, responsive gameplay, maintainable technical foundations and features that integrate smoothly into a real production pipeline.

Legal information

Privacy center

One permanent place for the privacy information of every released game and service.

Policies will be published per product

A privacy policy must match the actual game, its store listing and the services it uses. Rather than publishing a generic text that could be inaccurate, each policy will be added here after its data flows and third-party SDKs are reviewed.

Every final policy will have a stable, shareable link suitable for Google Play, the App Store and project websites.

Before publication

  • Game name and store links
  • Analytics, ads and other SDKs
  • Data collection and retention details
  • Developer contact and effective date
