## Information needed for each public privacy policy

- Exact game name, store URL and developer/publisher name.
- Contact email for privacy questions.
- Platforms and target audience, including whether children may use the game.
- Every SDK or external service: analytics, ads, attribution, crash reporting, login, payments and cloud storage.
- Categories of data collected, why it is collected, retention period and deletion/request process.
- Effective date and the legal jurisdiction or company details that must appear in the policy.
